## Sales Overview (KPIs & Trends)

- Total Revenue (KPI)
- Average Order Value (KPI)
- Revenue by Month (Line/Bar chart)
- Number of Orders per Day (Traffic)
- Orders Trend (Daily & Monthly delivered products – merged)

## Customer Insights

- Total Unique Customers
- Repeat Purchase Rate
- Top N customer cities (user demography)\*

## Product Performance

- Top N Best-Selling Products
- Products Never Purchased
- Top N purchased product category\*

## Delivery Performance

- Daily & Monthly trend of delivered products\*
- Average Delivery Time vs. Estimated Time
- On-Time Delivery Rate
- Daily status of the product purchase\*

## Marketing Funnel Insights

- Leads to Customer Conversion Rate
- Revenue by Marketing Channel

## Review & Customer Satisfaction

- Average Review Score
- Review Score Distribution

## Most Important:

- Revenue & AOV (KPIs).
- Orders Trend (Daily/Monthly).
- Top Product Categories & Best Sellers.
- Customer Demographics (Cities & States).
- Conversion Funnel (Leads → Customers → Purchases).
